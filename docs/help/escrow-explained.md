# How escrow works

SafeTrust uses escrow to hold funds while the stay is in progress.

## The guest pays first

When a guest books a listing, the payment is sent to an escrow account. The funds are not released immediately.

## The host delivers the stay

The stay is then completed according to the booking flow. Both sides can see the booking status and any follow-up actions.

## Funds are released once conditions are met

Once the stay is confirmed or resolved, the escrow flow releases the funds to the host or refunds the guest as needed.

## Why this helps

Escrow reduces the risk of payment disputes and gives both parties a clear, auditable trail during the transaction.
