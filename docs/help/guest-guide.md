# Guest guide

Use SafeTrust to browse listings, compare options, and book with confidence.

## Browse before you book

Open **/rent** and use the filters to narrow the results by destination, price, bedrooms, and property type. You can also use **Use my location** to sort by distance.

## Compare listings

Review the listing details, amenities, and location before you pick a place. If a property is a demo, the app will say so clearly.

## Book and pay

When you choose a listing, the app guides you through the booking flow and wallet connection. SafeTrust holds the funds in escrow until the stay is confirmed.

## What happens after checkout

You can track the booking status from the dashboard. If a payment or confirmation is delayed, check the status page or the [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) guide.

## Need help?

Start with [Getting started with SafeTrust](/help/getting-started) and [Finding a place on Rent](/help/using-rent-filters).
