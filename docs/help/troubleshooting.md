# Troubleshooting

## Location was denied

Your browser blocked location access. Open your browser's site settings for SafeTrust, allow **Location**, and reload the page. You can also skip location and pick a destination on **/rent**.

## Wallet not installed, or on the wrong network

Install a Stellar wallet, then open it and switch to the network SafeTrust is using. Return to SafeTrust and connect the wallet again.

## "We couldn't confirm the result"

The app could not confirm the payment or action yet. Wait a few minutes and check again before you try a second time, so you do not pay twice.

## Contact support

Use the support link in the site footer. Include what you were doing and the time it happened. Do not send your seed phrase or private key to anyone, including support.
