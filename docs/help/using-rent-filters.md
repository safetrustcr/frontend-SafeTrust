# Finding a place on Rent

Open **/rent** to browse listings.

## Location is optional

Click **Use my location** to sort by distance. If you prefer, skip it and pick a destination instead. Click **Clear location** to remove your location.

## Destinations

Choose a destination from the list, or choose **All of Costa Rica** to see every listing.

## Category chips

Tap a category chip to narrow the listings to that type of place.

## Filters

Click **Filters** to set:

- a minimum and maximum price;
- the number of bedrooms.

## Sort

Choose one of these from **Sort**:

- **Relevance**
- **Price: Low to High**
- **Price: High to Low**
- **Nearest** (available after you share your location)

## Reset

Click **Reset** to clear your destination, chips, filters, and sort.
